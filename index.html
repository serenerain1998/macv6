About Me

Passionate about creating digital experiences that make a difference in people's lives.

20+ Years Experience

I'm Melissa

I'm a UX/UI designer with over 20 years of experience creating user-centered digital solutions. I tend to thrive where design, technology, and strategy meet. I am passionate about learning and teaching something new every day — you'll never hear me say "I can't".

I'm passionate about building full blown UIs with sturdy UX bones and developing POCs that speak to the true intention of the design. I have led design systems from the ground up, guided engineering teams, and partnered with PMs and customers to create products that ship at scale.

My secret sauce is creating the perfect cocktail of cognitive psych and design which translates to products that pull on the natural instincts of users.

You'll often find me designing and building functional prototypes, building and scaling design systems, and working with engineering teams to bring designs to life. Whether it's a design system, a complex enterprise workflow, or a customer-facing app, my focus stays the same: make it simple, make it usable, and make it impactful.

I specialize in complex systems, accessibility, and creating inclusive designs that work for everyone. My work spans risk and compliance, healthcare and medical device technology, cybersecurity, logistics and supply chain, e-commerce, fintech, AI tooling, and enterprise applications.

My Design Philosophy

Over 20+ years, I've learned that great design isn't about following trends—it's about understanding how people think, see, and interact with the world.

01

Psychology-Driven Design

I design based on how brains actually work—respecting cognitive limits, leveraging natural perception, and creating interfaces that feel intuitive because they align with human thought processes.

Explore
02

Human-Centric Approach

Early in my career, I designed what I thought was beautiful. Users hated it. That taught me design isn't about me—it's about them. Every decision I make is filtered through one question: "Does this help or hinder the person using it?"

Explore
03

Design Thinking Process

I've watched projects fail when teams skip empathy. I've seen "impossible" challenges solved when we listen to users. My process is messy and iterative—I've gone back to research after testing revealed we were solving the wrong problem.
